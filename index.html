Operational Hazards

Operational Risk Protocols for Long-Duration Spaceflight (LDSE)

Space Radiation
Invisible to the human eye, space radiation is not only stealthy but considered one of the most hazardous aspects of spaceflight. It causes DNA damage and acute health effects.
Isolation and Confinement
Behavioral responses occur among groups of people far from Earth who are isolated and confined in a small space over a long period of time, leading to cognitive decline.
Distance from Earth
Instructions, new supplies, medical care, and more become increasingly challenging to receive from Earth as astronauts venture deeper into space. Telecommunication lag time is severe.
Gravity Fields
Astronauts' entire bodies - muscles, bones, inner ear, and organs - must adjust to the new gravities encountered on the space station or their spacecraft, as well as on the Moon and Mars.
Hostile/Closed Environments
Maintaining a safe ecosystem inside a spacecraft is essential; this means air quality, temperature, and microbial balance must be continuously monitored for crew safety.
